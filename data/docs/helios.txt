Helios Energy Corporation: Annual Overview
Helios Energy Corporation is a solar technology company headquartered in Austin. The company was founded by Maria Chen, who also serves as its chief executive officer. Helios is subject to the Clean Grid Act, which sets reliability standards for utility scale solar operators.
In 2021, HELIOS ENERGY CORP acquired Voltaic Labs, a Boston startup that builds battery management systems. The acquisition brought the Prism controller into the Helios product line. Helios also partners with Quanta Motors to provide home charging for electric vehicles. Helios competes with Sunforge Solar in the residential market.
