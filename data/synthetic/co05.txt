Cobalt Beacon Inc.: Company Profile
Cobalt Beacon Corporation partners with Crimson Ridge Corporation. The chief executive officer of COBALT BEACON is Marta Kowalski. COBALT BEACON supplies Crimson Bridge. Cobalt Beacon Corporation was founded by Omar Nakamura in 1972. The company employs about 6042 people. Cobalt Beacon Corporation is headquartered in Leeds. COBALT BEACON acquired Summit Canyon in 2005.
