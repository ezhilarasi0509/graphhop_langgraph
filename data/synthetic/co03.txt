Silent Harbor Inc.: Company Profile
Silent Harbor is headquartered in Perth. SILENT HARBOR acquired Iron Canyon in 2019. Silent Harbor Corporation was founded by Dev Rossi in 2010. The chief executive officer of SILENT HARBOR is Lars Silva. The company employs about 1938 people. Silent Harbor acquired Northern Harbor Corporation in 2012. SILENT HARBOR partners with Northern Meadow Inc.. Silent Harbor acquired Lunar Delta in 2009. Silent Harbor acquired Lunar Forge Inc. in 2001.
