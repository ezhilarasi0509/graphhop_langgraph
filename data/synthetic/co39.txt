Cobalt Lantern Inc.: Company Profile
COBALT LANTERN is headquartered in Turin. Cobalt Lantern was founded by Idris Castillo in 2006. The chief executive officer of COBALT LANTERN is Sofia Iyer. Cobalt Lantern supplies Willow Quarry Inc.. The company employs about 1270 people. Cobalt Lantern partners with Northern Ridge Corporation.
