Iron Bridge Inc.: Company Profile
The chief executive officer of Iron Bridge Inc. is Idris Haddad. IRON BRIDGE partners with Willow Beacon. IRON BRIDGE was founded by Jonas Adeyemi in 1966. Iron Bridge acquired Amber Orchard Inc. in 2010. Iron Bridge Corporation supplies Summit Delta. Iron Bridge is headquartered in Gdansk. The company employs about 6370 people.
