Silent Meadow Inc.: Company Profile
Silent Meadow Inc. partners with SILENT BEACON. SILENT MEADOW supplies Cobalt Lantern Inc.. The company employs about 7044 people. Silent Meadow was founded by Lena Brandt in 1981. Silent Meadow acquired Copper Bridge Inc. in 1998. The chief executive officer of Silent Meadow Corporation is Clara Silva. Silent Meadow is headquartered in Hanoi.
