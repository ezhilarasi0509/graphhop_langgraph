Silent Beacon Inc.: Company Profile
Silent Beacon Inc. acquired Copper Lantern Inc. in 2002. The company employs about 4550 people. Silent Beacon Corporation is headquartered in Denver. The chief executive officer of SILENT BEACON is Jonas Varga. Silent Beacon Corporation acquired Northern Delta in 2010. SILENT BEACON acquired Lunar Falcon in 2014. Silent Beacon Inc. partners with Silent Meadow. Silent Beacon Corporation acquired Silent Forge Corporation in 2022. Silent Beacon was founded by Lena Rossi in 2014.
