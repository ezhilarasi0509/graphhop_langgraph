Silent Quarry Inc.: Company Profile
Silent Quarry Inc. is headquartered in Lisbon. Silent Quarry Inc. partners with Northern Harbor Corporation. Silent Quarry Inc. was founded by Jonas Silva in 1986. Silent Quarry Corporation supplies Silent Forge Corporation. Silent Quarry acquired Crimson Ridge Inc. in 2017. SILENT QUARRY supplies Lunar Bridge. SILENT QUARRY acquired Northern Ridge Inc. in 2003. Silent Quarry Inc. acquired VELVET CANYON in 2020. Silent Quarry Inc. acquired Cobalt Beacon Corporation in 1997. The chief executive officer of Silent Quarry Corporation is Clara Castillo. The company employs about 2192 people.
