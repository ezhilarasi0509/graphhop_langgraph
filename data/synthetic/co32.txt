Copper Quarry Inc.: Company Profile
Copper Quarry Corporation partners with Willow Quarry Inc.. Copper Quarry Inc. is headquartered in Quito. The company employs about 8471 people. Copper Quarry supplies Willow Meadow Inc.. The chief executive officer of Copper Quarry is Marta Silva. COPPER QUARRY was founded by Idris Novak in 2007.
