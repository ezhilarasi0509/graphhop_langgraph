Northern Falcon Inc.: Company Profile
Northern Falcon Inc. partners with Lunar Delta Corporation. Northern Falcon acquired Copper Canyon Corporation in 2020. The chief executive officer of Northern Falcon is Omar Bauer. Northern Falcon Corporation acquired Granite Meadow Inc. in 2005. The company employs about 3916 people. NORTHERN FALCON acquired Silent Quarry in 2021. NORTHERN FALCON is headquartered in Quito. Northern Falcon Inc. acquired Iron Beacon Corporation in 2014. Northern Falcon acquired Silent Harbor Corporation in 2024. Northern Falcon Inc. was founded by Idris Duarte in 1966.
